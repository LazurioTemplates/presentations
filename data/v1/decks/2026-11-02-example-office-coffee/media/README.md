# Media of the sample deck

Made for this template; part of it under the MIT License in `LICENSE` at the
repository root. No stock footage, music or third-party image.

- `coffee-setup.mp4`: an 8-second animation without text and without sound
  (H.264, 1280 × 720), drawn frame by frame in the colours of the design
  system (`design-system/content/brand/colors/tokens.json`) and encoded with
  ffmpeg.
- `coffee-setup-poster.jpg`: its last frame, the poster of the video slide and
  what a printed deck shows instead of the video.
